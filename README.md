# Family Formals Planner

Plan the family photos at a wedding: enter the family once, get the best shot order, who steps in and out, and who is free to go. Keeps divorced parents apart, finishes grandparents and small kids first.

Use it: https://aiyoto-myth.github.io/family-formals/

Free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded. Your list stays in your browser; the share link carries the list inside the link itself.
